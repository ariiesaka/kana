# Kana study home screen

## Surface mode
Operate

## Goal
Help a beginner choose a reading-practice format quickly.

## Confirmed first choice
Multiple Choice, Matching, or Flashcards, ordered from more guidance to recall.

## Confirmed composition
A compact activity list.

## Copy style
Use lowercase for visible interface copy.

## Scope
Design the study home screen only. The practice activities and their session flows come later.

## Visual direction
Use the selected Quiet Reader direction in dark mode.
Keep the home screen minimalist and compact.

## Open decisions
- How learners choose hiragana, katakana, or mixed content after choosing a practice mode.
