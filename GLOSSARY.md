# Kana

A study tool that helps a beginner memorize hiragana and katakana well enough to read them fluently.

## Language

**Kana**:
A single hiragana or katakana character, such as あ or ア.
_Avoid_: letter, symbol, character (when the script matters)

**Script**:
One of the two kana writing systems, hiragana or katakana.
_Avoid_: alphabet, syllabary, mode

**Card**:
One kana in one script, with its own study history. あ and ア are two cards.
_Avoid_: item, question, flashcard (a flashcard is the format, not the unit)

**Group**:
A set of kana that share a starting sound, such as the k group か き く け こ. A group exists in both scripts. The vowels group is あ い う え お; ん belongs to the w group.
_Avoid_: row, set, level

**Learning order**:
The fixed sequence in which groups are introduced: vowels, k, s, t, n, h, m, y, r, w. The w group holds わ を ん. Katakana for a group is introduced once the same group's hiragana is reliable.
_Avoid_: level, curriculum

**Grade**:
The learner's own verdict on one card after seeing the answer: knew it or missed.
_Avoid_: score, rating, answer

**Box**:
A card's stage in the review schedule, from 1 (newest or just missed) to 4. Each box has a fixed wait before the card is due again: same day, 1 day, 3 days, 7 days. Knew it moves a card up one box; missed returns it to box 1.
_Avoid_: level, bucket, stage

**Due**:
A card whose wait has passed, so it is ready to be reviewed. A new card is due immediately.
_Avoid_: pending, overdue

**Reliable**:
A card is reliable at box 3 or above. A group is reliable when at least 80% of its cards are. Reliability unlocks the next group and, for hiragana, the same group's katakana.
_Avoid_: learned, mastered

**Study day**:
The day that boxes and the new-card cap count in. It rolls over at 4 a.m. local time, not midnight.
_Avoid_: calendar day

**New card**:
A card the learner has never graded. At most 5 are introduced per study day, mixed in among due reviews.
_Avoid_: unseen card

**Extra practice**:
A session on recently missed or newest cards when nothing is due. Grades are ignored, so it never changes boxes.
_Avoid_: cram, free play

**Flashcards**:
The practice format where the learner sees a card, recalls its reading, reveals the answer, then grades their own recall.
_Avoid_: quiz, test

**Session**:
One open-ended stretch of practice that the learner can leave at any point without losing progress.
_Avoid_: round, lesson, run
